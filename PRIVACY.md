# Privacy and data placement

The rule: **every kind of data has one home, and the automation enforces it.** This file is a starting point. Have your People or legal lead review it for your jurisdictions.

| Data | ATS | Approval thread | Project tool | Onboarding channel | Open hiring channel | Automation can read |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Candidate name | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ (after signing) | ✅ (after signing) | ❌ | ✅ |
| Interview feedback | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Pay, bonus, equity, relocation | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Role, team, manager, start date | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Role only | ✅ |
| Equipment and app needs | — | — | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| ID numbers, bank details, home address | HR system only | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Background check results | Vendor + ATS only | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Pipeline counts by stage | ✅ | — | — | — | ✅ (counts only) | ✅ |

## Rules the automation follows

1. It never reads pay, ID numbers, bank details, addresses, dates of birth, tax forms, benefit choices or background-check results, even where it has access.
2. It never contacts a candidate or new hire. It drafts the welcome email; a person reviews and sends it.
3. It never changes a start date, an ATS stage or anything a person typed.
4. It never names the person who skipped a process step in a channel.
5. Reporting in shared channels is aggregate: counts, medians and step names, not people.
6. If it can't read a source cleanly, it writes nothing.

## Fairness

Tracking Day-1 readiness and time between steps by team and hiring channel (job board, referral, executive direct) shows whether some new hires consistently get a worse start than others. Report it in aggregate, and only where group sizes are large enough that no individual can be identified.
