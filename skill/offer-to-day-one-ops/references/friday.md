# Friday (after the onboarding sync). No DMs on Fridays.

## A. Health board

- **At a glance:** Offers (in progress by step, accepted last 90 days, acceptance rate) · Speed (offer stage to Day 1, approval to sent, candidate decision time) · Day 1 (starting in next 30 days, share fully ready on Day 1, top blocker type).
- **Time per step:** median business days over the last 90 days, with a trend marker (🟢 at or under target, 🟡 up to 50% over, 🔴 more than 50% over). With fewer than 5 hires, list each hire's actual days instead of a median.
- Update the "last updated" line. Never edit the decisions log or the measurement notes.

## B. #onboarding digest (at most one message)

Post only if something changed this week, someone starts within 14 days with open items, or a row needs a fix. Otherwise post nothing.

Sections, only those with content: Offers in progress · Accepted / declined · Starting in the next 14 days (open items only) · Needs a fix. Tag a person only if they own something overdue or due before next Friday, once per digest.

## C. #hiring roll-up (at most one message)

1. Read #hiring since last Friday only.
2. One line per open role: "<Role> · <Status> · <next step or blocker>". No reply this week: "<Role> · no update". **No candidate names, feedback or pay**, even if a reply contains them.
3. Tag no one. If nothing changed anywhere, post nothing.
