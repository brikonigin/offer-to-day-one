# Onboarding checklist template

Due dates are counted in **business days** from the anchor, so they move automatically when the start date moves. Replace owners with your own roles.

| # | Task | Owner | Anchor | Offset | Tracker column |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send onboarding note to new hire (Email 1) | People Ops | Offer signed | +0 | — |
| 2 | Send manager kickoff (Email 2) | People Ops | Offer signed | +0 | — |

| 4 | Follow up on background and reference checks; right-to-work verification | People Ops | Offer signed | +1 | Background check |

| 10 | Review and send welcome email (Email 3) | Recruiting lead | Start date | −1 | — |
| 11 | Record Day-1 outcome | Hiring manager | Start date | +1 | Day-1 outcome |

## Start-date rules

- Default to a **Tuesday**. Monday starts lose the Friday-before buffer for IT and People Ops.
- Never the business day before or after a holiday weekend.
- Use one published holiday calendar (for example, a bank-holiday calendar) so everyone counts business days the same way.
- The minimum offer-to-start window is the largest negative offset plus the time a laptop takes to ship. Publish it so hiring managers don't promise dates the team can't meet.
